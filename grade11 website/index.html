What Is Gig Work?

Gig work is short-term, flexible work where you are paid per task, per trip or per delivery — instead of a fixed monthly salary. You choose when you work, where you work, and how much you work.

Instead of one permanent employer, gig workers connect with customers through apps and platforms like ride-hailing, food delivery and courier services. You are essentially your own boss: no office, no fixed hours, no long job interviews.

In South Africa, gig work has become a lifeline for students, job seekers and anyone who needs an income while looking after family or building a business.

Getting a Gig Job Is Simple

No CV piles, no months of waiting. Most people can start earning within a week by following these four easy steps.

Choose Your Gig

Pick the type of work that suits you — driving people, delivering food, transporting goods or couriering parcels.

Meet the Basic Requirements

You usually need a smartphone, a bank account, and the right licence or vehicle for the job. See our Skills page for the full list.

Sign Up on the App

Register on the platform of your choice, upload your documents, and pass a quick background check.

Start Earning

Once approved, switch the app online and accept your first job. You get paid weekly or even daily on some platforms.

Types of Gig Work

Here are the four most popular gig jobs in South Africa — with pictures of each one.

Ride-Hailing

Uber / E-hailing Driving

Use your own car (or a rental) to drive passengers around the city. You earn per trip plus tips, and you choose your own hours.

Food Delivery

Food Delivery

Deliver restaurant meals and groceries with apps like Mr D, Uber Eats and Checkers Sixty60. A scooter, motorbike or bicycle is all you need.

Transport Services

Transport Services

Move people or goods locally — school runs, airport shuttles, moving furniture or shuttling staff. Great if you have a van or a roomy car.

Courier Service

Courier & Parcel Delivery

Collect and deliver parcels, documents and online-shopping orders for courier companies and e-commerce stores, on your own schedule.
